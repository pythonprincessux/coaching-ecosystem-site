Business & Professional Leadership

When This Path May Be Right for You

You may recognize yourself here if:

  • You rarely consider yourself before making decisions, and self-neglect has quietly become your normal.
  • You carry the weight of everything, feeling responsible for holding it all together.
  • Your days are driven by urgency, deadlines, and other people’s needs rather than deliberate choices.
  • You’ve become disconnected from what you need, what you value most, and what your current capacity allows.
  • You realize something needs to change, even if you can’t yet define what that change looks like.
